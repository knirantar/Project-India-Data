INTRODUCTION


Findings from the current reporting period indicate that protection risks affecting displaced populations across Gaza continue to be shaped by 
deepening food insecurity, deteriorating shelter and sanitation conditions, and increasing barriers to accessing humanitarian assistance and essential services
. Communities reported growing difficulties in accessing food following the suspension of community kitchens, severe shortages of safe water, worsening environmental health conditions linked to open sewage and infestation, and continued overcrowding in deteriorating shelters. These conditions are contributing to heightened exposure to disease, erosion of dignity, and growing psychosocial distress, particularly among children, older persons, persons with disabilities, and individuals with pre-existing health conditions.


Communities also highlighted increasing challenges related to access to humanitarian assistance, including barriers linked to civil documentation, digital assistance mechanisms, and reduced humanitarian access in areas located near the so-called “yellow” and “orange” lines. At the same time, the presence of explosive remnants of war (ERW) in and around displacement sites continues to pose serious safety risks, particularly for children and communities with limited awareness of reporting and survivor assistance pathways.


The Protection Monitoring System (PMS) continues to provide timely and systematic evidence on protection risks affecting displaced populations across Gaza. Through a combination of 
Key Informant Interviews (KIIs)
 and 
Focus Group Discussions (FGDs)
, the system captures both quantitative trends and qualitative community perspectives to inform protection analysis and response planning. Community feedback during this reporting period highlights increasing reliance on 
negative and constrained coping mechanisms
, including reduced food consumption, growing debt, and greater dependence on humanitarian assistance and informal support networks to meet basic needs amid prolonged displacement and deteriorating living conditions. This snapshot presents an overview of protection risk severity scores, evolving risk trends since the start of monitoring in 
August 2024
, and key protection concerns raised by affected communities during the most recent reporting period.
