DTM launched a new round of ETT on 14 June 2026 using an updated methodology to monitor displacement driven by conflict, as well as the climatic impacts of the El Niño phenomenon, which is expected to trigger significant displacement toward the end of the year. DTM teams collect data in five districts: Baidoa, Dayniile, Diinsoor, Doolow, and Kahda.


ETT is a crisis-based tool that tracks sudden displacement triggered by specific events or emerging crises. Its objective is to support the prioritization of humanitarian response and enable partners to deliver rapid assistance. It includes information on new arrivals, the number and demographics of IDPs, reasons for displacement, intentions, humanitarian assistance received, and priority needs, among others. Based on previous shock induced displacement patterns, the humanitarian community expects continued movement toward urban areas in search of services. Consequently, ETT coverage focuses on main urban centers and surrounding villages within each assessed district.
