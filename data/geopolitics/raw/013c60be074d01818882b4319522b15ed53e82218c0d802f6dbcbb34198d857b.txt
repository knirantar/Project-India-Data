Data  collected  by  Insecurity  Insight  shows  that  between  07  October  2023  and  23  June  2026,  at  least  3,354  incidents of  violence  against  or  obstruction  of  access  to  health  care  took  place  across  the  occupied  Palestinian  territory.

Other  resources  Insecurity  Insight  has  released  a  report examining  how  the  conflict  between  Iran,  Israel,  Lebanon  and  the  US  has  impacted  emergency  medical  services  across  the  Greater  Middle  East  region.   
Key  findings  ●  The  highest  number  of  incidents  affecting  emergency  medical  services  were  recorded  in  Lebanon  ●  Nearly  three  quarters  of  incidents  were  attributed  to  Israeli  forces  ●  Sequential  strikes  on  emergency  responders  were  documented  in  Iran,  Iraq  and  Lebanon.  ●  Emergency  responders  in  southern  Lebanon  reported  delays  and  heightened  risks  during  rescue  operations.

This  document  comprises  threats  and  violence  as  well  as  protests  and  other  events  affecting  the  delivery  of  and  access  to  health  care.  It  is  part  of  the  Attacks  on  Health  Care project  by  Insecurity  Insight.  It  is  prepared  from  information  available  in  local,  national  and  international  news  outlets  and  online  databases.  The  incidents  reported  are  not  a  complete  nor  a  representative  list  of  all  events  that  affected  the  provision  of  health  care  and  have  not  been  independently  verified  through  ground  investigations.
